# Exercise 2 -- Copy a 1-minute tape

Play one continuous minute of Morse from your trainer (or a practice WAV you
generated offline). Copy by ear onto paper or a text file. Do not pause-scrub
through the minute for this exercise -- treat it like a short QSO burst.

Record:

Date: ____________  Trainer / file: ________________________________

Character WPM: ______  Effective WPM: ______

Characters sent (count or trainer report): ______

Characters correct: ______  Accuracy percent: ______

Hardest characters this minute: ________________________________

What you will change tomorrow (spacing, speed, or rest): 
___________________________________________________________________________

Optional: paste a one-line summary into day 5 or day 7 `notes` in your practice log.
