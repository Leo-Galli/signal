# Link diagram sketch (Chapter 1 exercise)

Draw boxes and arrows on paper or in any editor. Label each box.

1. Source (for this chapter: a commercial FM broadcast transmitter you do not own)
2. Propagation path (free space / building walls -- qualitative only)
3. Your antenna
4. Your receiver (RTL-SDR + host software)
5. Your ears / headphones / speakers after demodulation

Mark the direction of power flow. Mark "receive only" on your station.
Do not invent power levels. Leave watts/dBm blank until you have a real measurement method.
