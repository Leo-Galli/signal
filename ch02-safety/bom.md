# Bill of materials -- Chapter 2

Power and antenna checklist card; fuse and reverse-polarity diode on a 5 V rail.

| Item | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Multimeter | DC voltage and continuity (diode-test helpful) |
| Fuse holder + fuse | Rate from wire/holder limits and measured load; do not invent a value |
| Reverse-polarity diode | Sized for 5 V DC input current; read the diode datasheet |
| Lab PSU or USB power bank | Meter the 5 V output under a small load before trusting it |
| Breadboard or protoboard | For the fuse-and-diode teaching rail |
| Hookup wire | Short runs; mark polarity |
| Notebook + printed checklist card | From `worksheets/field_checklist_card.md` |

No transmitter. No RF PA on this rail for the chapter verify.
